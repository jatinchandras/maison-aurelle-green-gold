Good to know

Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Weekdays are usually open within a week. For weekends and colour services, book two weeks ahead.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, whenever a chair is free. Calling ahead helps us give you a time.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please let us know 24 hours before. Late changes may carry a small fee for colour services.

Are your products vegan?

Most are. We use plant-based, sulphate-free ranges and can tell you what's in each one.
